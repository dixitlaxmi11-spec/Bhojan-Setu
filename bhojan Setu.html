How BhojanSetu Works

A seamless 4-step pipeline designed for speed, food safety, and maximum social impact across neighborhoods.

1. Donor Uploads Food

Marriage caterers, restaurants, or community halls list surplus portions with safe pickup time windows.

2. NGOs Notified

Verified local ashrams, night shelters, and community food banks within your city radius receive instant alerts.

3. Volunteer Pickup

A nearby community volunteer accepts the task, dispatches, and triggers live landing alerts upon arrival.

4. Families Fed

Fresh meals are successfully distributed to daily wage earners, slum communities, and shelter residents.
